Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

In short

PingAll does not collect, store or transmit any personal data to me or to third parties. There is no user account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Your network

PingAll sends pings to the devices of the network you enter, looks up their hostnames (via your DNS server and Bonjour) and, on the Mac, reads their MAC addresses from the system to show the device vendor. A port scan connects to a single device only when you start it. All results, the history and the settings stay locally on your device and are not uploaded anywhere.

Internet check

To check whether the internet is reachable, PingAll pings the internet check target you set (8.8.8.8, a public Google DNS server, by default). Only a ping packet is sent; it contains no personal data.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. I do not receive your payment details. The app only asks the App Store whether a purchase is active.

Email support

If you email me at support.tb@icloud.com, I use your address and message only to answer your request. Email is received via Apple iCloud Mail. I do not share it and delete it once it is no longer needed.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). GitHub may store technical data such as your IP address in server logs to operate the service securely. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. The site itself sets no cookies and uses no analytics.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase and restrict the processing of your data, and to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. As the app collects no data, this in practice only concerns emails you send me.
